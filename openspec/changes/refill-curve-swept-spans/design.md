# Design

The curve field can change outside adjacent spans. ClayCore blends the round stroke chain, while swept profiles use the guide's total arc length and parallel-transported frames. A bound derived solely from moved controls cannot guarantee a complete refill. Before replacing a guide, mark its old node extent; afterward mark the new node extent and drain both. A zero displacement does no work. This deliberately spends more bricks per append until the field and cache expose a proven local edit bound.

The regression hashes quantized cached mesh positions before and after a forced full refill across joins and radii. Append and zero-drag checks cover their paths. The rendered fixture checks pixel agreement after a drag.
